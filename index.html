02 / RESEARCH NOTES

Forecasts meet physics.

Read the frozen protocol ↗
01

Commit early

Schedules are fixed at 10:00 Danish time on the previous day. DK1 uses Nordcast’s frozen ridge model; DK2 uses its boosted median. Their original choices stay locked.

02

Respect the battery

A mixed-integer optimizer enforces capacity, power and energy balance. Charge and discharge efficiency are each 95%. Binary operating modes prevent simultaneous flows.

03

Reconcile every euro

The fixed schedule settles at actual day-ahead prices. Fees and wear are deducted interval by interval. Weekly, idle and hindsight policies provide transparent comparisons.

What this experiment can—and cannot—tell us

This is a retrospective research replay on an already examined July–September 2026 sample, not prospective validation. Forecasts and revised historical prices are imported from the pinned Nordcast archive. No weather forecasts or new predictive models are added here.

The early commitment is a modeling choice. After day-ahead prices are published, those prices could be used directly. We assume full fills and omit market access, imbalance costs, ramp rates, self-discharge, reserve obligations, capital cost, grid tariffs and taxes. Wear is a fixed cost proxy, not a battery aging model.

The asset-size selector scales power and capacity together at constant duration. It scales the same schedule and cash flows linearly, without reoptimizing efficiency, costs, or market impact. The daily CSV is calculated from seven-decimal display schedules; the full-precision compressed journal is the authoritative accounting artifact.
